FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Still unsure which plan fits? Start with Explorer. You can upgrade in one click whenever you are ready.

Is Explorer really free?

Yes. Explorer gives you daily questions, a trial module, practice exams and basic analytics at no cost, for as long as you like.

What is Doubtster, and how do credits work?

Doubtster is an AI tutor inside ItaloStudy Global. Asking questions and getting written answers is unlimited. Credits are only used to make explanation videos: one credit makes one video, and Global includes 10.

What happens when my 10 credits run out?

You can keep chatting with Doubtster for free. If you want more videos, you can get more credits on Doubtster.

What is the difference between Global and Target Batches?

Global is your daily practice platform: questions, modules, mocks, analytics, admission support and Doubtster. Target Batches are teacher-led courses with live and recorded lectures, valid until your exam date. Many students use both.

Do I need a separate plan for each exam?

No. One Global plan gives you access to every exam on ItaloStudy: IMAT, CEnT-S, TIL-I, Bocconi, IELTS, SAT and TOLC. No extra charges, no add-ons.

Monthly or quarterly?

Same plan, same features. Quarterly is billed every three months and costs less per month, which suits students who are a few months from their exam.

Which exams do you cover?

CEnT-S, IMAT, TOLC, TIL-I, Bocconi, IELTS and SAT. One Global plan covers all of them.
