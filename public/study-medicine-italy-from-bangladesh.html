Why Bangladeshi Students Should Consider Italy

Italy is an emerging and increasingly popular destination for Bangladeshi medical students. While many Bangladeshi families traditionally look at China, Russia, or the Philippines for affordable medical education abroad, Italy offers a significantly better value proposition with a globally superior degree.

Key Advantages Over Other Destinations

  • EU-recognized degree — Unlike degrees from China or Russia, an Italian medical degree lets you practice across all 27 EU countries without additional exams
  • English-taught programs — No need to learn a new language before starting (Italian is optional and taught alongside the program)
  • Extremely low tuition — Public universities charge EUR 150-4,000/year based on family income, far less than Bangladeshi private medical colleges
  • World-class training — Italy has some of the oldest and most prestigious medical schools in the world (Bologna, founded 1088)
  • Safety and quality of life — Compared to other popular MBBS-abroad destinations, Italy offers superior safety, healthcare, and living standards
  • Growing Bangladeshi community — Italy has a well-established Bangladeshi diaspora (approximately 150,000 Bangladeshis in Italy), providing cultural support and community

Bangladeshi Community Advantage

Italy is home to one of the largest Bangladeshi diaspora communities in Europe, particularly in Rome, Venice, Naples, and Palermo. This means access to Bangladeshi food, cultural events, community organizations, and a support network that makes the transition much smoother than in many other European countries.

BMDC Recognition of Italian Medical Degrees

This is the most common concern for Bangladeshi families, and the answer is positive:

  • WHO listing: Italian medical schools are listed by the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS), which is a primary criterion for BMDC recognition
  • BMDC licensing exam: After graduating from an Italian medical school, you must pass the BMDC licensing examination to practice in Bangladesh — this is the same requirement for graduates from any foreign medical school
  • EU advantage: An Italian degree allows you to practice across all EU countries without additional exams, giving you a massive career advantage over degrees from non-EU countries
  • Verify your university: Before applying, confirm your target Italian university is on the current BMDC-recognized list. Major public universities (Milan, Bologna, Rome, Pavia, Turin, Naples) are typically recognized
Important: Always check the latest BMDC list of recognized foreign medical universities before finalizing your application. Contact BMDC directly or visit their website for the most current information. The recognition status can change, and it's your responsibility to verify before enrollment.

Student Visa from Bangladesh (Dhaka)

Bangladeshi students apply for an Italian student visa through the Italian Embassy in Dhaka or through VFS Global Bangladesh.

Step-by-Step Visa Process

Step 1 — Gather Documents

University acceptance letter, HSC certificate (attested by Education Board + Ministry of Education + Ministry of Foreign Affairs), proof of financial means (EUR 6,500/year — approximately BDT 8,12,500 in bank statement), health insurance, accommodation proof.

Step 2 — Document Attestation

Get your educational documents attested through the chain: Education Board → Ministry of Education → Ministry of Foreign Affairs → Italian Embassy. This process can take 2-3 weeks, so start early.

Step 3 — Book VFS Appointment

Book an appointment through VFS Global Bangladesh for the Italian student visa. During peak season (July-August), slots fill quickly. Book as early as possible once you have your acceptance letter.

Step 4 — Submit Application

Attend your VFS appointment in Dhaka with all documents. Biometrics will be collected. The application is forwarded to the Italian Embassy for processing.

Step 5 — Processing (2-4 Weeks)

The Italian Embassy reviews your application. They may call you for a brief interview. Processing typically takes 2-4 weeks. Apply by early August to receive your visa before October classes.

Step 6 — Collect Visa & Travel

Collect your passport with the Type D student visa from VFS. Book your flight to Italy (Dhaka → Milan/Rome via Dubai or Doha). Apply for your Permesso di Soggiorno within 8 days of arriving in Italy.

Document Checklist for Bangladeshi Students

  • HSC Certificate & Marksheet — From Education Board (original + attested copies)
  • SSC Certificate & Marksheet — Original + attested copies
  • Full Attestation Chain — Education Board → Ministry of Education → Ministry of Foreign Affairs → Italian Embassy
  • Sworn English Translation — Of all educational documents (by a certified translator)
  • Valid Passport — At least 12 months validity beyond your intended stay
  • IMAT/CEnT-S Score Report — Official exam results
  • University Acceptance Letter — From the Italian university
  • Financial Proof — Bank statement showing EUR 6,500+ (~BDT 8,12,500), or sponsorship letter from parents with their bank statements
  • Health Insurance — Valid for Italy, covering at least the first year
  • Accommodation Proof — University housing, rental agreement, or host declaration
  • Passport Photos — 4 biometric photos (35x45mm, white background)
  • Universitaly Enrollment Confirmation — Printed from your Universitaly.it account

Scholarships for Bangladeshi Students

Italian universities and government bodies offer generous financial aid that can make studying in Italy cheaper than studying in Bangladesh:

DSU (Diritto allo Studio Universitario)

The main Italian financial aid system, based on family income. For most Bangladeshi families, the income threshold qualifies for significant support:

  • Full tuition waiver (EUR 0 tuition)
  • Free university housing
  • Meal vouchers for university canteen (EUR 2-3/meal)
  • Monthly stipend of EUR 200-350 (~BDT 25,000-43,750/month)

University Merit Scholarships

High CEnT-S/IMAT scores (55+ points) can earn merit-based tuition reductions of 30-100%. Universities like Bologna, Padua, and Milan have specific programs for international students from developing countries.

Italian Government Scholarships (MAECI)

The Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs offers scholarships to Bangladeshi citizens through bilateral agreements. These cover tuition, a monthly allowance (EUR 900/month), and health insurance. Applications are typically through the Italian Embassy in Dhaka — check their website annually for deadlines.

Invest Your Talent in Italy (IYT)

A program by the Italian Trade Agency that provides scholarships to students from selected countries including Bangladesh. It covers tuition and provides a monthly stipend.

Why Rome, Naples, and Bari are Popular with BD Students

These cities have lower living costs (EUR 500-700/month vs EUR 900-1,200 in Milan), established Bangladeshi communities with halal food shops and restaurants, lower cutoff scores making admission more accessible, and generous DSU financial aid programs. Bari in particular has very affordable housing and one of the lowest cutoffs among Italian medical schools.

Life in Italy as a Bangladeshi Student

Halal Food & Cultural Needs

Italy's Bangladeshi community has established halal butchers and grocery stores in most major cities. You'll find:

  • Halal meat shops in every university city (look for "Macelleria Halal" or Bangladeshi/Pakistani shops)
  • South Asian grocery stores with familiar spices, rice, dal, and cooking ingredients
  • Bangladeshi and Indian restaurants (especially in Rome's Esquilino area, Naples, and Venice)
  • University canteens that offer vegetarian options daily
  • Mosques and prayer spaces — Italy has mosques in all major cities

Bangladeshi Student Associations

Connect with fellow Bangladeshi students through:

  • University-specific BD student WhatsApp groups (ask on social media before arriving)
  • Bangladesh Community Italy (Facebook groups with 50,000+ members)
  • Bangladesh Embassy cultural events in Rome
  • Eid celebrations and national day events organized by the Bangladeshi community

Part-Time Work

International students in Italy can work up to 20 hours/week during the academic year and full-time during breaks. Common student jobs:

  • University tutoring (EUR 10-15/hour)
  • Restaurant/hospitality work (EUR 8-12/hour)
  • Online freelancing (Upwork, Fiverr — Bangladeshi students are already strong in this)
  • Translation services (Bengali-English-Italian)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Bangladeshi students study medicine in Italy?
Yes. Bangladeshi students can study medicine in Italy by passing the IMAT or CEnT-S entrance exam. Italy offers 6-year medical degrees taught entirely in English at public universities, with tuition as low as EUR 150/year. A growing community of Bangladeshi students is already studying medicine across Italian universities.
Is an Italian medical degree recognized by BMDC?
Italian medical degrees from recognized universities are eligible for BMDC (Bangladesh Medical and Dental Council) registration after passing the BMDC licensing examination. Italy's medical education is globally respected and its degrees are recognized by WHO. Contact BMDC directly for the current list of recognized Italian institutions before enrolling.
What is the total cost to study medicine in Italy from Bangladesh?
Annual costs range from EUR 8,000-15,000 (approximately BDT 10-18 lakh) including tuition (EUR 150-4,000), housing (EUR 300-600/month), and living expenses. With DSU scholarships, this can drop to EUR 2,000-5,000/year (BDT 2.5-6 lakh). Compare this to private medical colleges in Bangladesh at BDT 15-25 lakh/year.
Do I need IELTS to study medicine in Italy?
Most Italian universities do NOT require IELTS for English-taught medical programs. The CEnT-S/IMAT exam is conducted in English, which serves as proof of English proficiency. A few universities may ask for B2-level English certification, but this is the exception rather than the rule.
How do I get a student visa for Italy from Bangladesh?
Apply at the Italian Embassy in Dhaka (or VFS Global Bangladesh). You need: university acceptance letter, proof of financial means (EUR 6,500/year or equivalent in BDT), health insurance, accommodation proof, and educational documents with full attestation chain (Education Board → Ministry of Education → Ministry of Foreign Affairs → Italian Embassy). Processing takes 2-4 weeks.
Which exam should Bangladeshi students take — IMAT or CEnT-S?
Both exams grant access to English-taught medical programs in Italy. The CEnT-S is the newer exam format. Check which exam your target universities accept. Both cover Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Logical Reasoning, and General Knowledge. ItaloStudy provides preparation materials and mock tests for both exams.
