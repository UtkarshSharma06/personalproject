Why Turkish Students Choose Italy for Medicine (Neden İtalya?)

Italy has become one of the top destinations for Turkish pre-med students, and for good reason. With tuition fees as low as EUR 150/year at public universities, world-class medical training recognized across the EU, and a cultural proximity that makes adaptation easier, Italy offers an unbeatable value proposition for Turkish families.

Turkey currently sends the second-largest cohort of international students to Italian medical schools, behind only India. The bilateral relationship between Turkey and Italy in education has strengthened significantly, with several cooperation agreements making the transition smoother than ever.

Türkiye-İtalya Eğitim İlişkisi (Turkey-Italy Education Relationship)

Turkey and Italy signed a bilateral education agreement covering mutual recognition of academic qualifications. While YÖK equivalence is still required for practice in Turkey, the process has been streamlined for Italian medical degrees, with approval rates exceeding 95% for graduates of accredited Italian universities.

Key Advantages for Turkish Students

  • Dramatically lower cost — Italian public university tuition (EUR 150-4,000/yr) is a fraction of Turkish private medical school fees (200,000-500,000 TRY/yr)
  • EU-recognized degree — Practice medicine anywhere in the European Union after graduation without additional exams
  • No entrance exam fee burden — The CEnT-S exam fee is only EUR 30-50 (approximately 1,050-1,750 TRY), compared to tens of thousands TRY for Turkish private university entrance
  • Strong Turkish community — Established student communities in Milan, Rome, Bologna, Pavia, and Naples with active WhatsApp groups and mentorship
  • Cultural similarity — Mediterranean lifestyle, similar food culture, and Italy's welcoming attitude toward Turkish students
  • Scholarship access — DSU (Diritto allo Studio Universitario) grants cover tuition, housing, and meals for eligible students

CEnT-S Exam Overview for Turkish Students (CEnT-S Sınavı Hakkında)

The CEnT-S (Centro Esame Nazionale Test di Scienze) replaced the IMAT as the primary entrance exam for English-taught medical programs in Italy. For Turkish students, CEnT-S is the gateway to public university medical schools taught in English.

YÖK Equivalence (Denklik) Process

YÖK Denklik Süreci — İtalyan Tıp Diplomasının Türkiye'de Tanınması

If you plan to practice medicine in Turkey after graduating from an Italian university, you'll need YÖK (Yükseköğretim Kurulu) equivalence. Here's what you need to know:

  • Recognition rate: Over 95% of Italian medical degrees receive YÖK equivalence
  • Processing time: 3-6 months after application
  • Required documents: Original diploma, transcript, internship completion certificate, curriculum details
  • Additional requirement: You may need to pass the TUS (Tıpta Uzmanlık Sınavı) for specialization in Turkey
  • EU advantage: If you decide to practice in any EU country instead, no equivalence process is needed — your degree is automatically recognized
Pro Tip: Many Turkish students choose to specialize in Italy or another EU country after graduation, then return to Turkey with both the specialization and the base degree. This path often provides better training opportunities and higher earning potential.

Step-by-Step Application Timeline (Başvuru Takvimi)

Here's your complete timeline from CEnT-S registration to your first day in an Italian medical school:

January - March 2027 — Prepare for CEnT-S

Start focused preparation. Take diagnostic mock tests on ItaloStudy to identify weak areas. Turkish students should prioritize General Knowledge and Logical Reasoning, as science subjects typically require less prep time.

March - April 2027 — Register for CEnT-S

Register for the CEnT-S exam through the official portal. Pay the exam fee (EUR 30-50). You can take the exam at test centers in Istanbul or Ankara, or at Italian consulate-arranged locations.

April - May 2027 — Take the CEnT-S Exam

Sit the 100-minute exam. Remember: +1.5 for correct, -0.4 for wrong, 0 for blank. Target score: 40+ for competitive programs, 50+ for top universities like Milan and Bologna.

May - June 2027 — Universitaly Pre-Enrollment

Create your account on Universitaly.it and complete the pre-enrollment process. Select up to 3 university preferences. Upload your high school diploma (lise diploması) and transcript.

June - July 2027 — Prepare Documents

Get your diploma and transcript translated by a sworn translator (yeminli tercüman). Apostille your documents at the relevant Turkish governorship. Obtain a Dichiarazione di Valore from the Italian Consulate in Istanbul or Ankara.

July - August 2027 — Results & Enrollment

CEnT-S results are published. If accepted, confirm your enrollment at the assigned university. Pay the first tuition installment (often EUR 156 for the first year).

August - September 2027 — Visa Application

Apply for a Type D student visa at the Italian Consulate in Istanbul or Ankara. Required: acceptance letter, proof of financial means (approximately EUR 6,500/year or family guarantee), accommodation booking, health insurance.

September - October 2027 — Arrive & Start!

Fly to Italy, obtain your Permesso di Soggiorno (residence permit) within 8 days of arrival, open an Italian bank account, and register for your Codice Fiscale. Classes typically begin in the first week of October.

Scholarships & Financial Aid (Burslar)

Italian universities offer significant financial support to international students, and Turkish students are well-positioned to benefit:

DSU (Diritto allo Studio Universitario)

This is the main financial aid system. Based on family income (ISEE declaration), it can cover:

  • Full tuition waiver (EUR 0 tuition)
  • Free university housing (posto letto)
  • Meal card for university canteen (approximately EUR 2-3 per meal)
  • Monthly stipend of EUR 200-350

Turkish families with moderate incomes typically qualify for partial or full DSU benefits. The ISEE threshold varies by region but is approximately EUR 24,000-26,000 family income.

University Merit Scholarships

Many Italian universities offer merit-based scholarships for high CEnT-S scores. Students scoring above 55-60 points may receive tuition reductions of 30-100%. Universities like Bologna, Padua, and Turin have specific programs for high-achieving international students.

Turkish Government Scholarships

While most Turkish government scholarships (YTB) focus on incoming students, some programs support Turkish students studying abroad. Check with the Turkish Ministry of Education for current programs available for medical students in Italy.

Italian Student Visa from Turkey (İtalya Öğrenci Vizesi)

Turkish citizens need a Type D student visa to study in Italy. The application is submitted at the Italian Consulate General in Istanbul or the Italian Embassy in Ankara.

Visa Application Steps

  1. Book an appointment — Through the Italian Consulate's online booking system (prenotami.esteri.it). Book early as slots fill quickly in summer.
  2. Prepare documents — Acceptance letter, financial proof (EUR 6,500/year), accommodation booking, health insurance, return flight booking or declaration
  3. Attend interview — Brief interview at the consulate. They may ask about your study plans and Italian university
  4. Processing time — 2-4 weeks. Apply by late July to receive your visa before October classes start
  5. Permesso di Soggiorno — Within 8 days of arriving in Italy, apply for your residence permit at the local post office (Poste Italiane) using the kit available there
Important for Turkish Students: The financial proof requirement is EUR 6,500/year. This can be shown as a bank guarantee from a parent's Turkish bank account, converted at the current TRY/EUR rate. A guarantor letter from parents (with their bank statements) is accepted. Some students also use blocked accounts at Italian banks.

Life in Italy as a Turkish Student

Turkish students consistently report high satisfaction studying in Italy. Here's what to expect:

Turkish Student Communities

Every major Italian university city has an active Turkish student association. These groups organize:

  • Welcome events for new arrivals (oryantasyon)
  • Study groups and exam preparation sessions
  • Cultural celebrations (bayramlar, Turkish nights)
  • Housing assistance and shared apartment listings
  • WhatsApp and Telegram groups for daily questions

Finding Halal Food

Italian cities with large student populations have increasing options for halal food. Turkish/Middle Eastern restaurants and shops (often run by the local Turkish community) are found in most university cities. University canteens also typically offer vegetarian options. Cities like Milan, Rome, and Bologna have the widest halal food access.

Travel Between Turkey and Italy

Direct flights operate daily between Istanbul and Milan, Rome, Bologna, and Naples. Budget airlines (Pegasus, Ryanair) offer one-way tickets from EUR 30-80 (1,050-2,800 TRY), making visits home affordable. Flight time is approximately 2.5-3 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Turkish students take the CEnT-S exam?
Yes. Turkish students can take the CEnT-S exam to apply to English-taught medical programs in Italy. Turkey is one of the top source countries, with approximately 3,500+ Turkish students currently enrolled in Italian medical schools. The exam can be taken at test centers in Turkey or at Italian consulate-arranged locations.
Is YÖK equivalence required for Italian medical degrees?
Italian medical degrees (Laurea in Medicina e Chirurgia) are recognized across the EU. For practice in Turkey, you must apply for YÖK equivalence (denklik) after graduation, which involves submitting your diploma and transcript for evaluation. The approval rate exceeds 95% for graduates of accredited Italian universities. If you choose to practice within the EU, no equivalence is needed.
What is the total cost for Turkish students to study medicine in Italy?
Public university tuition ranges from EUR 150 to EUR 4,000 per year based on ISEE. With living costs of EUR 600-1,000/month, total annual expenses run EUR 8,000-16,000 (approximately 280,000-560,000 TRY). Scholarships and DSU aid can reduce this by 50-80%, bringing costs down to EUR 2,000-5,000/year (~70,000-175,000 TRY).
Do I need IELTS or TOEFL as a Turkish student?
Most Italian universities do not require IELTS/TOEFL for English-taught medical programs. The CEnT-S exam itself is in English, which serves as proof of English proficiency. However, a few universities may request a B2 certificate — check your target university's specific requirements before applying.
How long is the CEnT-S exam and what does it cover?
The CEnT-S exam lasts 100 minutes and consists of 60 multiple-choice questions covering Biology (18 questions), Chemistry (12), Physics and Mathematics (10), Logical Reasoning (8), and General Knowledge (12). Correct answers earn +1.5 points, wrong answers deduct -0.4 points. Turkish students typically have strong foundations in the science sections.
When should Turkish students start preparing for CEnT-S 2027?
Ideally 6-9 months before the exam. Turkish high school graduates (especially from Fen Lisesi and Anadolu Lisesi) typically have strong foundations in biology, chemistry, and physics, so 4-6 months of focused CEnT-S preparation is often sufficient.
