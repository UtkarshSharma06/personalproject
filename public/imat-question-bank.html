Sample Questions from Our Bank

Biology — Cell Biology — Medium
Which of the following correctly describes the function of the rough endoplasmic reticulum?
  • A) Synthesis of lipids and steroid hormones
  • B) Synthesis and processing of proteins destined for secretion
  • C) Detoxification of drugs and alcohol
  • D) Production of ATP through oxidative phosphorylation
  • E) Packaging and sorting of proteins into vesicles
Explanation: The rough endoplasmic reticulum (RER) is studded with ribosomes, which synthesize proteins. Proteins made on the RER are typically destined for secretion, membrane insertion, or delivery to other organelles. Option A describes smooth ER function, C is smooth ER, D is mitochondria, and E is the Golgi apparatus.
Logical Reasoning — Critical Thinking — Hard
A study found that students who drink coffee before exams score 15% higher on average. A school administrator concludes that providing free coffee before exams would improve student performance. Which of the following, if true, most weakens this conclusion?
  • A) The study was conducted at a prestigious university
  • B) Tea contains similar amounts of caffeine
  • C) Students who drink coffee before exams also tend to have studied more the night before
  • D) Some students experience anxiety from caffeine
  • E) The study had a sample size of 500 students
Explanation: Option C identifies a confounding variable — the coffee-drinking students may score higher because they studied more, not because of the coffee itself. This is a classic correlation vs. causation error. The administrator's conclusion assumes coffee causes better performance, but C suggests a lurking variable (study habits) could explain the correlation.
