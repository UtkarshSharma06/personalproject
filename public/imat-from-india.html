Why Indian Students Are Choosing Italy Over Private Medical Colleges

Every year, over 5,500 Indian students apply for the IMAT — and that number is growing by 20% annually. The reason is simple: Italian public universities offer a world-class English-taught MD (equivalent to MBBS) at a fraction of what Indian private medical colleges charge.

Consider this: while a private MBBS seat in India costs ₹50 lakh to ₹1.5 crore over 5 years, an Italian public university charges between ₹1.5 lakh and ₹3.5 lakh per year — with many students paying even less through income-based fee waivers. The 6-year Italian MD degree is recognized by the WHO, listed in WDOMS, and accepted worldwide.

Unlike destinations like Ukraine, Russia, or China, Italy gives you a degree taught entirely in English at government-funded universities ranked among the world's best. After graduation, you can practice in any EU country or return to India after clearing the FMGE.

Key Fact for Indian Students

You do NOT need NEET to apply for IMAT. The IMAT (International Medical Admissions Test) is a completely separate exam conducted by Cambridge Assessment. Indian students with a Class 12 PCB qualification can directly apply.

Eligibility for Indian Students

To sit for the IMAT and enrol in an Italian medical university, Indian students need:

  • Class 12 (PCB) from a recognized board — CBSE, ICSE, State Boards are all accepted. Minimum 60% aggregate recommended (some universities require higher).
  • Valid passport — Must be valid for at least 18 months from your planned arrival in Italy.
  • Age requirement — You must be at least 17 years old by December 31 of the admission year. No upper age limit.
  • English proficiency — No IELTS/TOEFL required since IMAT itself is in English. Some universities may ask for a B2 English certificate, but this is rare.
  • Universitaly pre-enrollment — You must pre-enroll through the Universitaly portal and obtain a reference number before sitting for IMAT.
  • Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) — Your Class 12 marks need to be validated by the Italian Embassy/Consulate in India.

₹40,000 — ₹70,000 /month

Includes accommodation (₹15-35K), food (₹10-15K), transport (₹3-5K), and personal expenses (₹5-10K). Cities like Pavia and Bari are cheaper than Milan or Rome.

₹35 — ₹55 Lakh total

Compare this to ₹50L-1.5Cr for private MBBS in India, or ₹25-40L for countries like Ukraine/Philippines that lack EU recognition.

DSU Scholarship: Indian students with ISEE equivalent below €23,000 can apply for the DSU (Diritto allo Studio Universitario) scholarship — worth €5,000-€6,000/year plus free accommodation, free canteen meals, and book allowances. Approximately 40% of international students receive some form of financial aid.

Step-by-Step IMAT Application Process from India

Follow this timeline for the IMAT 2027 cycle (exam in September 2027):

1

Start Preparation (January - March 2027)

Begin IMAT-specific preparation alongside your Class 12 board exams. Focus on Biology and Chemistry first, then add Logical Reasoning and General Knowledge. Use ItaloStudy's question bank to practice exam-style MCQs daily.

2

Universitaly Pre-Enrollment (April - July 2027)

Create an account on universitaly.it, select your preferred universities (up to 3 choices), upload your passport, Class 12 marksheet, and photo. Pay attention to the pre-enrollment deadline — it's typically in July. Save your reference number.

3

Declaration of Value (May - July 2027)

Contact the Italian Embassy in New Delhi or the Consulates in Mumbai/Kolkata/Chennai to get your Dichiarazione di Valore. This validates your Indian Class 12 qualification. Start early — processing can take 4-6 weeks during peak season.

4

IMAT Registration (July - August 2027)

Register for IMAT on the Cambridge Assessment portal when registration opens (usually July). Choose your test centre — IMAT is held in multiple Indian cities or you can take it online. Pay the exam fee (approximately €110 / ₹10,000).

5

Take IMAT Exam (September 2027)

The exam is held in mid-September. 60 questions in 100 minutes. Aim for 40+ out of 90 to be competitive for top universities. Results are published within 2-3 weeks.

6

Enrollment & Rankings (October 2027)

Universities publish rankings based on IMAT scores and your preference list. If you're assigned a seat, you'll receive an acceptance letter. Confirm your enrollment and pay the first tuition instalment.

7

Student Visa Application (October - November 2027)

Apply for a Type D student visa at VFS Global. Book your appointment early — slots fill up fast during this period. You'll need your acceptance letter, proof of funds (€6,500+ in bank), health insurance, and accommodation proof.

8

Arrive in Italy (November - December 2027)

Fly to Italy, apply for your Permesso di Soggiorno (residence permit) within 8 days of arrival, set up your Codice Fiscale (tax code), open an Italian bank account, and begin your medical journey!

Italy Student Visa from India: VFS Process

After receiving your university acceptance letter, apply for the Type D (long-stay) student visa at VFS Global centres. Here are the India-specific details:

VFS Global Centres in India

You can apply at any of the following cities: New Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chandigarh, Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, and Kochi. Each centre is linked to a specific Italian Consular jurisdiction.

Documents Required

  • Valid passport — at least 2 blank pages, valid for 18+ months
  • University acceptance letter — original from the Italian university
  • Proof of accommodation — hostel booking, rental agreement, or declaration of hospitality
  • Financial proof — Bank statement showing minimum €6,500 (~₹6 lakh) for one year, or scholarship letter
  • Health insurance — Valid in Italy, covering at least €30,000 in medical expenses
  • Flight itinerary — Return ticket or onward travel proof
  • Class 12 marksheet and certificate — Attested and apostilled
  • Dichiarazione di Valore — From the Italian Embassy
  • 2 passport-size photos — Recent, white background, 35x45mm
  • Visa application form — Filled and signed
Important: Book your VFS appointment at least 45 days before your planned travel date. During peak season (September-November), slots can be fully booked weeks in advance. Visa processing typically takes 15-30 working days. Apply as soon as you receive your acceptance letter.

Indian Student Community in Italy

Italy has a thriving Indian student community, especially in cities like Milan, Bologna, Rome, and Pavia. Here's what the community offers:

  • Indian Student Associations — Most major universities have active Indian student groups that organise cultural events, study groups, and support networks for newcomers.
  • WhatsApp & Telegram Groups — City-specific groups where seniors help freshers with accommodation, SIM cards, banking, and daily life questions.
  • Indian Grocery Stores — Available in Milan, Rome, Bologna, Turin, and other major cities. You can find dal, rice, spices, and other essentials.
  • Religious Places — Hindu temples and Gurudwaras exist in Milan and Rome. Many cities have regular prayer gatherings.
  • Indian Restaurants — Multiple options in every major Italian city, plus many students cook Indian food together.
  • Festival Celebrations — Diwali, Holi, Eid, and other festivals are celebrated together by Indian student communities across Italy.

FMGE: Returning to Practice in India

If you plan to return to India after completing your Italian MD, you'll need to pass the FMGE (Foreign Medical Graduate Examination):

  • Conducted by NBE (National Board of Examinations) twice a year (June and December)
  • 300 MCQs covering all medical subjects, 150 marks to pass (50%)
  • Italian medical graduates historically have a strong FMGE pass rate due to rigorous European training
  • After passing FMGE, you receive a provisional registration to practice in India
  • Alternatively, many Indian graduates choose to stay in the EU where their degree is directly valid — working in Italy, Germany, UK, or other European countries

NMC (National Medical Commission) Recognition

Italian medical universities are listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS) and recognised by India's NMC. Verify your chosen university's listing on the WDOMS website before applying. All 14 Italian public universities offering English-taught medicine are currently listed.

Frequently Asked Questions — Indian Students

Can Indian students give IMAT without NEET?
Yes. IMAT is completely independent of NEET. Indian students do not need a NEET score to apply for English-taught MBBS programs in Italy. IMAT is administered by Cambridge Assessment and is the sole entrance exam for international students at most Italian public medical universities.
What is the cost of studying medicine in Italy for Indian students?
Italian public universities charge tuition fees between €150 and €4,000 per year based on family income (ISEE calculation). With living expenses of €500-€800/month, the total annual cost ranges from ₹5-10 lakh — significantly less than Indian private medical colleges. Many students qualify for fee waivers and DSU scholarships covering accommodation and meals.
Is the Italian MBBS degree valid in India?
Yes. Italian medical degrees are recognized worldwide and listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS). To practice in India after graduation, you must pass the FMGE (Foreign Medical Graduate Examination) conducted by the National Board of Examinations. The 6-year Italian MD program is equivalent to the Indian MBBS.
How do I apply for the Italy student visa from India?
After securing admission through IMAT, apply for a Type D student visa at VFS Global centres in New Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, or Chandigarh. Required documents include the university acceptance letter, proof of accommodation, financial means (€6,500+ in bank), health insurance, and passport. Processing takes 2-4 weeks.
What is the IMAT exam pattern in 2027?
IMAT consists of 60 multiple-choice questions in 100 minutes. The sections are: General Knowledge (12 questions), Logical Reasoning (8 questions), Biology (18 questions), Chemistry (12 questions), and Physics & Mathematics (10 questions). Scoring: +1.5 for correct, -0.4 for wrong, 0 for unanswered. The exam is held in September.
Which Italian universities are best for Indian students?
Popular choices among Indian students include the University of Milan, University of Bologna, University of Pavia, University of Rome La Sapienza, University of Padua, and University of Turin. These universities have large international student communities, strong support services, and well-established English-taught medicine programs.
