CEnT-S Exam Fees 2027: Complete Cost Breakdown, Payment Methods & Fee Waivers

Everything you need to know about CEnT-S exam costs — from the registration fee to hidden expenses most guides don't mention.

CEnT-S Registration Fee: Quick Answer

EUR 30–50
CEnT-S Exam Registration Fee (2027)
Exact amount confirmed when CISIA opens registration — typically March-April

The CEnT-S (Centro di Eccellenza Test — Scienze) exam registration fee is set by CISIA, the consortium that administers the test. The fee has ranged between EUR 30-50 in recent years and covers the exam itself, results processing, and score reporting to your selected universities.

Use a credit/debit card or PayPal for instant confirmation. Bank transfers can take 2-3 days, and your registration is only confirmed after payment clears. If you're near the deadline, use instant payment methods to avoid last-minute stress.

Fee Waivers and Reductions

While the CEnT-S exam fee itself doesn't typically have waivers, there are several ways to reduce your overall costs:

University Tuition Fee Waivers

Many Italian public universities offer significant tuition reductions based on family income:

  • ISEE below EUR 13,000: Full tuition waiver at most public universities
  • ISEE EUR 13,000-25,000: Reduced tuition (typically EUR 500-2,000/year)
  • ISEE above EUR 25,000: Standard tuition (up to EUR 4,000/year at public universities)

Non-EU students provide equivalent income documentation from their home country.

DSU Scholarships (Regional Scholarships)

Regional DSU (Diritto allo Studio Universitario) scholarships can cover:

  • Full tuition waiver
  • Free university housing
  • Meal vouchers (mensa)
  • Monthly stipend (EUR 200-400/month in some regions)
💰 Real Example: A student from India with family income under EUR 15,000 equivalent can study medicine in Italy for virtually zero tuition. With a DSU scholarship covering housing and meals, the only out-of-pocket costs are personal expenses (~EUR 200-300/month). Total 6-year cost: approximately EUR 15,000-20,000 — less than one year at many Indian private medical colleges.

Refund Policy

Important things to know about CEnT-S exam fee refunds:

  • Standard policy: Registration fees are generally non-refundable once payment is confirmed
  • Cancellation window: Some exam sessions may offer a cancellation window (typically 7-14 days before the exam)
  • Medical emergencies: Documented medical emergencies may qualify for refund or reschedule — contact CISIA directly
  • Force majeure: If CISIA cancels the exam due to extraordinary circumstances, full refunds are issued
  • No-show: Not showing up for the exam does not qualify for a refund

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the CEnT-S exam cost?
The CEnT-S exam registration fee is approximately EUR 30-50, paid through the CISIA portal. The exact fee is published when registration opens (typically March-April). This covers the exam, score processing, and reporting to universities.
Is there a fee waiver for CEnT-S?
The exam fee itself typically doesn't have waivers, but university tuition can be fully waived based on family income (ISEE below EUR 13,000). DSU regional scholarships can cover tuition, housing, and meals. The exam fee is a small fraction of the overall cost.
Can I get a refund if I don't take the exam?
Generally, the registration fee is non-refundable. Medical emergencies with documentation may qualify for a refund or reschedule. Contact CISIA directly for exceptional circumstances. Not showing up does not qualify for a refund.
What additional costs should I budget?
Beyond the EUR 30-50 exam fee, budget for: document apostille (EUR 50-200), sworn translations (EUR 100-300), Declaration of Value (EUR 0-100), travel to exam center (varies), and student visa (EUR 50-116). Total realistic budget for non-EU students: EUR 500-800.
Do I pay separately for each university?
The CEnT-S exam fee covers the test itself, and your score is valid for all participating universities. You pay once for the exam. However, individual universities may charge enrollment or application fees during the admission phase.
