Universitaly Pre-Enrollment Guide 2027: Complete Step-by-Step Process

By ItaloStudy Editorial Team — Italy admissions & entrance exam specialists  |  Last updated: November 2026  |  About us

The definitive guide to navigating Universitaly pre-enrollment for international students. Avoid the mistakes that delay 40% of applicants every year.

What is Universitaly Pre-Enrollment?

Universitaly (universitaly.it) is the official Italian Ministry of Education (MUR) portal that all non-EU international students must use to apply for Italian universities. Pre-enrollment (pre-iscrizione) is the mandatory first step — without it, the Italian embassy won't process your student visa.

Think of Universitaly as the gateway: it connects you to the Italian embassy, the university, and the visa office. Completing it correctly and on time is critical — delays here cascade into missed visa appointments and lost university seats.

⚠️ Critical: Missing the Universitaly deadline means you cannot apply for a student visa, even if you pass the IMAT or CEnT-S exam. Every year, hundreds of students miss their chance because they start the Universitaly process too late. Begin at least 2-3 months before the deadline.

2027 Timeline: Key Dates for Universitaly

March-April 2027: Prepare Documents

Request Declaration of Value from Italian embassy. Get diploma apostilled/legalized. Prepare translations. This step takes 2-8 weeks.

April-May 2027: Portal Opens

Universitaly typically opens for 2027-2028 academic year applications. Create account immediately and start filling your application.

June-July 2027: Submission Deadline

Most programs have a July deadline for pre-enrollment. IMAT/CEnT-S programs may have specific windows. Submit well before the deadline.

July-August 2027: Embassy Verification

The Italian embassy verifies your documents. This can take 2-6 weeks. Respond promptly to any requests for additional documents.

September 2027: Entrance Exam + Visa

Take the IMAT/CEnT-S/TOLC exam. If accepted, finalize your student visa application. The visa process takes another 2-4 weeks.

October 2027: Enrollment + Travel

Complete final enrollment at your university. Travel to Italy with your student visa, valid passport, and all original documents.

Step-by-Step: How to Complete Universitaly Pre-Enrollment

1

Create Your Universitaly Account

Visit universitaly.it and click "Register" (Registrati). You'll need:

  • A valid email address (use one you check regularly — all communications come here)
  • Personal details matching your passport exactly
  • Your fiscal code (codice fiscale) if you have one — you can get this from the Italian embassy or generate it online

Verify your email immediately. The verification link expires in 24 hours.

2

Complete Your Profile

Fill in all personal information, including:

  • Full name as on passport (no nicknames or abbreviations)
  • Date and place of birth
  • Nationality and country of residence
  • Current address and contact details
  • Academic background (high school name, graduation year, grades)
3

Select Your University and Program

Search for your target program. For English-taught medical programs, look for:

  • "Medicina e Chirurgia" (Medicine and Surgery) — English language
  • You can typically select 1-3 university preferences (varies by year)
  • Double-check the campus and language of instruction
4

Upload Required Documents

This is where most students get stuck. Upload clear, legible scans of all required documents (see checklist below). All documents must be in PDF format, under 2MB each.

5

Submit and Monitor Your Application

After submitting, your application goes to the Italian embassy in your country for verification. Check your Universitaly dashboard and email regularly — the embassy may request additional documents. Typical verification takes 2-6 weeks.

Complete Document Checklist

Documents Required for Universitaly Pre-Enrollment

  • ☐ Valid passport — must be valid for at least 18 months from application date
  • ☐ High school diploma — original, with apostille or legalization (Hague convention countries use apostille)
  • ☐ Official transcript — final grades for all years of secondary education
  • ☐ Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) — issued by the Italian embassy in your country, certifying your qualification is equivalent to Italian standards
  • ☐ Sworn translation — official Italian translation of your diploma and transcript (by a certified translator)
  • ☐ Passport-sized photos — 2-4 photos meeting visa photo requirements
  • ☐ Proof of language proficiency — English proficiency certificate (IELTS/TOEFL) if required by the university
  • ☐ Application fee receipt — proof of payment for the application/exam fee (if applicable)
  • ☐ Codice Fiscale — Italian fiscal code (can be generated at the embassy or online)
💡 Pro Tip — Declaration of Value: The Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) is the most time-consuming document to obtain. Start this process at least 6-8 weeks before the Universitaly deadline. Contact your nearest Italian embassy or consulate immediately — some embassies have long processing queues, especially during peak application season (April-July).

Country-Specific Requirements

For Indian Students

  • 12th grade mark sheet and certificate must be apostilled by the MEA (Ministry of External Affairs)
  • Declaration of Value from the Italian Embassy in New Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, or Chennai
  • NEET qualification may be required for some medical programs (check specific university requirements)
  • Processing time at Indian embassies: typically 4-6 weeks

For Turkish Students

  • Lise diploması (high school diploma) with apostille from the Governor's office
  • YKS/TYT scores may be required as proof of university eligibility
  • Declaration of Value from the Italian Embassy in Ankara or Consulate in Istanbul
  • Processing time: typically 3-5 weeks

For Pakistani Students

  • Intermediate/A-Level certificate with attestation from IBCC and MOFA
  • Equivalence certificate from IBCC (Inter Board Committee of Chairmen)
  • Declaration of Value from the Italian Embassy in Islamabad
  • Processing time: typically 4-8 weeks

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Starting too late: Begin the Declaration of Value process in February-March, not June
  2. Name mismatches: Your name on Universitaly must match your passport exactly — no abbreviations, middle names must be included
  3. Poor document quality: Blurry or incomplete scans get rejected. Use a flatbed scanner, not a phone camera
  4. Missing translations: All non-Italian/English documents need sworn translations
  5. Wrong program selection: Make sure you select the English-taught version of the program, not the Italian one
  6. Ignoring embassy communications: Check your email and Universitaly dashboard daily — delayed responses can void your application
  7. Not keeping copies: Keep both digital and physical copies of every document you submit

After Universitaly: What Happens Next?

If You Pass the Entrance Exam

  1. Check rankings: IMAT/CEnT-S results are published on the CINECA/university website. Check if you're admitted to your preferred university.
  2. Accept your place: If offered a seat, confirm your acceptance within the deadline (usually 3-5 days).
  3. Apply for student visa: With your acceptance letter and Universitaly confirmation, apply for a Type D student visa at the Italian embassy.
  4. Complete final enrollment: After arriving in Italy, complete enrollment at the university's Segreteria Studenti within 30 days.
  5. Apply for Permesso di Soggiorno: Within 8 days of arrival, apply for your residence permit at the local post office.

If You're on the Waiting List

Don't panic. IMAT rankings have multiple reallocation rounds. Many students get admitted during the 2nd or 3rd reallocation as others drop their seats. Stay enrolled on Universitaly and monitor the rankings closely.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Universitaly and why is it required?
Universitaly is the official Italian government portal (MUR) for international students. Pre-enrollment is mandatory for non-EU students who need a student visa. It connects you to the Italian embassy, the university, and the visa process. Without completing Universitaly, your visa application will not be processed.
When does Universitaly open for 2027?
Universitaly typically opens between April and July each year. For 2027-2028 admissions, expect it to open around April-May 2027. Create your account early and prepare documents in advance so you can submit immediately when it opens.
What documents do I need?
You need: valid passport, apostilled high school diploma, official transcript, Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) from the Italian embassy, sworn translations, passport photos, proof of English proficiency, and the application fee receipt. Country-specific requirements may apply.
Can EU students skip Universitaly?
EU/EEA students don't need Universitaly for visa purposes, but may still need to register on it for certain centralized admission programs (like IMAT). Check your specific university's requirements. EU students generally apply directly to the university.
How long does the Declaration of Value take?
Processing time varies by country: 2-4 weeks in EU countries, 4-6 weeks in India, 3-5 weeks in Turkey, and 4-8 weeks in Pakistan. Start this process as early as possible — it's the most common cause of application delays.
