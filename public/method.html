Preparation isn't a straight line; it's a series of tactical shifts. Here is what your typical timeline looks like when you use ItaloStudy the right way.

The Tactical Bible

How to actually use this platform to guarantee a seat in your top-choice university.

Rule #01
Stability Over Speed

Don't try to solve 100 questions in a day and then burn out for a week. Aim for 2 hours of deep, high-quality work every single day. The algorithm favors consistency; so should you.

Rule #02
The Review is the Study

If you get a question wrong, don't just look at the answer. Spend 5 minutes dissecting why you missed it. Was it a logic trap, or a knowledge gap? Use our community Q&A to bridge that gap instantly.

Rule #03
The Saturday Ritual

Every Saturday morning, simulate the actual exam. No phone, no music, no snacks. This builds the "exam stamina" that keeps you calm when the real clock starts ticking.
