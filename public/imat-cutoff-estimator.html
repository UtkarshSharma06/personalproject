Understanding IMAT Cutoff Scores

The IMAT cutoff score is the minimum score required for admission to a specific Italian medical university. Unlike many standardized tests, there is no universal "pass" mark — instead, each university admits students based on ranking, with available seats determining how far down the ranking they go.

Cutoffs vary dramatically between universities. In 2026, the highest EU cutoff was approximately 50.2 at Milan Statale, while the lowest was around 28.5 at Bari. This means that scoring 35 on the IMAT could get you into several universities while leaving you well below the threshold at the most competitive ones.

How We Calculate Cutoff Predictions

Our prediction model uses a weighted moving average of the last 3 years of cutoff data, combined with a trend adjustment factor. Recent years receive higher weight than older ones, reflecting the evolving competitive landscape:

Prediction Formula

Weighted Average = (Year N × 50%) + (Year N-1 × 30%) + (Year N-2 × 20%)
Trend Factor = Average year-over-year change × 0.7
Predicted Cutoff = Weighted Average + Trend Factor
Range = Prediction ± 2.5 (accounts for exam difficulty variation)

Factors That Influence IMAT Cutoffs

Several variables affect cutoff scores each year. Understanding these helps contextualize predictions and prepare for different scenarios:

📊

Exam Difficulty

A harder exam produces lower scores across the board, reducing cutoffs. Years with especially tricky general knowledge sections tend to have 2-4 point lower cutoffs.

👥

Applicant Numbers

More applicants means more competition for the same seats. IMAT applicant numbers have grown 8-12% annually since 2020.

🎓

Available Seats

Universities may increase or decrease their English-medium medicine seats. More seats at a university directly lowers its cutoff.

📈

Preparation Quality

As IMAT prep resources improve (including platforms like ItaloStudy), the overall score distribution shifts upward, pushing cutoffs higher.

🌍

Geopolitical Factors

Visa policy changes, exchange rates, and political stability affect international applicant pools, shifting demand between universities.

🔄

Preference Patterns

Students list universities in preference order. Northern universities are traditionally more popular, concentrating higher-scoring students.

University Tiers: Which Schools to Target

Italian medical universities can be grouped into tiers based on their typical IMAT cutoffs. Knowing your target tier helps you set realistic score goals and choose backup options:

Tier 1: Elite (Cutoff 48+)

Milan Statale, Bologna, and Pavia consistently have the highest cutoffs. These universities are located in desirable Northern Italian cities with strong research programs and international reputations. If you're targeting these schools, aim for a score of 52+ to have a comfortable margin.

Tier 2: Competitive (Cutoff 40-48)

Rome La Sapienza, Padua, Turin, and Florence fall in this range. These are excellent universities with lower cost of living than Milan. A score of 44-48 puts you in a strong position for these programs.

Tier 3: Moderate (Cutoff 32-40)

Naples Federico II, Rome Tor Vergata, and several others sit in this tier. These universities offer high-quality medical education with more accessible cutoffs. A score of 36-40 should secure admission.

Tier 4: Accessible (Cutoff below 32)

Bari, Messina, and Catania typically have the lowest cutoffs. Don't confuse accessibility with quality — these are accredited medical schools with excellent faculty. They're great options for students who want to study in Italy and can build strong careers from any Italian medical degree.

Strategic Tip: Always list at least 3-4 universities across different tiers in your preference order. Many students who target only Milan end up with no placement when they could have gotten into an excellent program elsewhere. A flexible preference list maximizes your chances.

EU vs. Non-EU Cutoff Differences

The IMAT operates separate quotas for EU and non-EU candidates. This creates two distinct sets of cutoffs at each university:

  • EU Candidates: Generally face higher cutoffs due to larger applicant pools and more available seats. The data in our tool reflects EU cutoffs.
  • Non-EU Candidates: Have separate, usually smaller quotas. Cutoffs can be either higher or lower than EU cutoffs depending on the specific university and the nationality breakdown of applicants.
  • Country-specific quotas: Some universities allocate seats by nationality within the non-EU pool, which can create very different cutoffs for different nationalities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the expected IMAT cutoff for 2027?
Based on historical trends, the average EU minimum cutoff for IMAT 2027 is predicted to be between 43-46 points. However, top universities like Milan Statale may require 50+ while less competitive programs may admit students with 28-35 points. Select a university in our tool above for specific predictions.
Which Italian medical university has the lowest IMAT cutoff?
Historically, universities like Bari, Messina, and Catania have had the lowest IMAT cutoffs. In 2026, Bari had a minimum EU cutoff of around 28.5. However, lower cutoffs don't mean lower quality — these are excellent universities with strong medical programs and full accreditation.
Is the IMAT cutoff different for EU and non-EU students?
Yes. EU and non-EU students compete in separate pools with different quotas. EU students typically have more available seats but face larger applicant pools. Non-EU cutoffs vary significantly — some may be lower, while at competitive universities they can be comparable to EU cutoffs.
How accurate are IMAT cutoff predictions?
Our predictions based on weighted historical trends are generally accurate within ±3-5 points. The main variables that affect accuracy are exam difficulty (which varies year to year), total number of applicants, and changes in university quotas. Always use predictions as guidance and prepare for a range of scenarios.
Do IMAT cutoffs go up every year?
IMAT cutoffs have generally trended upward over the past 5-6 years, reflecting increased competition and better-prepared applicants. However, they can fluctuate based on exam difficulty — a harder exam produces lower scores across the board, temporarily reducing cutoffs.
What IMAT score do I need for Milan Statale?
Milan Statale consistently has the highest IMAT cutoff. In 2026, the EU minimum was around 50.2. For 2027, we estimate you'll need approximately 50-54 points to secure admission. Aim for 55+ to be safe. Use our score calculator to see how your practice scores compare.
