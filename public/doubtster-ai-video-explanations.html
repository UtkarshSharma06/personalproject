How it explains

One question. Six moves.

Scroll through a real Doubtster video, frame by frame. This is how it took apart a biology question.

01

Starts with your question

"Why is DNA replication semiconservative?" Written up top, so you always know what is being solved.

02

Rules out the wrong answers

Each wrong option gets crossed out, with the reason why. That is how you learn to spot traps.

03

Lays out what you know

What we know, what we need, and the method. Before a single calculation.

04

Works it step by step

Drawn out on screen, old strand in blue, new strand in red, narrated as it goes.

05

Lands the answer

One parental strand plus one new strand, in every new DNA molecule. Clear and boxed.

06

Flags the common mistake

The answer students most often pick by mistake, and why it does not hold up.
