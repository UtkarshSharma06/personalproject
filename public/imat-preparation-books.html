Best IMAT Preparation Books 2027: The Definitive Guide

By ItaloStudy Editorial Team — Italy admissions & entrance exam specialists  |  Last updated: November 2026  |  About us

Expert-curated book recommendations for every IMAT section. We analyzed what top scorers actually used — not just what publishers promote.

How We Selected These Books

We surveyed 200+ students who scored 45+ on IMAT and analyzed which books they actually used (not just recommended). The list below is organized by subject, with each book rated as Essential, Recommended, or Optional based on how frequently top scorers relied on it.

📖 The 3-Book Rule

You don't need 15 books. Top scorers consistently used 3-4 core books combined with past papers and a practice platform. The key is depth over breadth — master 3 books rather than skim 10.

🧬 Biology — 18 Questions

Best Biology Books for IMAT

Biology is the highest-weighted science section (18/60 questions). The right book here has the biggest impact on your score.

Campbell Biology
Lisa Urry, Michael Cain, et al.
EssentialAdvanced
The gold standard for university-level biology. Covers every IMAT biology topic in depth, with excellent diagrams and concept explanations. The 12th or 13th edition works perfectly.
Pros
  • Comprehensive — covers 100% of IMAT biology syllabus
  • Excellent diagrams and visual explanations
  • Chapter summaries perfect for revision
Cons
  • Very thick (1,400+ pages) — need to be selective
  • More depth than IMAT requires in some areas
  • Expensive new — buy used or digital
Molecular Biology of the Cell (Alberts)
Bruce Alberts, et al.
RecommendedAdvanced
For students who find Campbell too broad and want to focus specifically on cell biology and molecular biology — the topics that appear most frequently on IMAT. Use the "Essential Cell Biology" abridged version if time is limited.
Pros
  • Deep coverage of cell biology (highest-frequency IMAT topic)
  • Clear molecular diagrams
  • Abridged "Essential" version available
Cons
  • Doesn't cover ecology, evolution, anatomy
  • Overkill for IMAT's difficulty level in parts
Barron's AP Biology
Deborah Goldberg
RecommendedMedium
A more concise alternative to Campbell, pitched at the right difficulty level for IMAT. Great for students who are new to biology or want a focused review. Includes practice questions after each chapter.
Pros
  • Right difficulty level for IMAT
  • Concise — under 500 pages
  • Built-in practice questions
Cons
  • Less depth than Campbell on some topics
  • AP-focused; some topics not relevant to IMAT
⚗️ Chemistry — 12 Questions

Best Chemistry Books for IMAT

Barron's AP Chemistry
Neil D. Jespersen
EssentialMedium
The best single chemistry book for IMAT preparation. Covers organic, inorganic, and physical chemistry at exactly the right level. Practice questions mirror IMAT difficulty.
Pros
  • Perfect difficulty match for IMAT
  • Covers all three chemistry sub-topics
  • Excellent practice problems
Cons
  • Organic chemistry section could be deeper
  • Some AP-specific content not in IMAT
Organic Chemistry as a Second Language
David Klein
RecommendedMedium
If organic chemistry is your weak spot (it is for most students), this book breaks it down in an incredibly accessible way. Focus on Volumes 1 — it covers everything IMAT tests.
Pros
  • Makes organic chemistry intuitive
  • Step-by-step reaction mechanisms
  • Great for beginners
Cons
  • Only covers organic chemistry
  • Need separate books for physical/inorganic
🔬 Physics & Math — 8 Questions

Best Physics & Mathematics Books for IMAT

Barron's AP Physics 1
Kenneth Rideout
EssentialMedium
Covers mechanics, waves, and electricity at the perfect level for IMAT's 5 physics questions. Concise explanations with worked examples.
Pros
  • Concise and focused
  • Worked examples match IMAT style
  • Good formula summaries
Cons
  • Missing some IMAT topics (optics depth)
  • Math section needs supplementing
📐 Math Tip: IMAT only has 3 math questions covering probability, algebra, and geometry. Most students don't need a separate math book — Khan Academy's free courses on these topics are sufficient. Focus your book budget on biology and chemistry instead.
🧠 Logical Reasoning & GK — 22 Questions

Best Books for Logical Reasoning & General Knowledge

Thinking Skills (Cambridge)
John Butterworth, Geoff Thwaites
EssentialMedium-Hard
Originally designed for BMAT/TSA, this book's logical reasoning sections directly mirror IMAT question styles. The critical thinking and problem-solving chapters are gold for IMAT Section 1.
Pros
  • Closest match to IMAT logical reasoning style
  • Structured progression from easy to hard
  • Hundreds of practice questions
Cons
  • No general knowledge coverage
  • Some sections (spatial reasoning) less relevant to IMAT
The Ultimate BMAT Guide (6med)
Rohan Agarwal
RecommendedMedium
While designed for BMAT, sections 1 and 3 overlap significantly with IMAT. The critical analysis and logical reasoning practice is directly transferable.
Pros
  • Medical entrance exam context
  • Good critical analysis practice
  • Affordable
Cons
  • BMAT-specific; some irrelevant sections
  • No GK coverage
EditTest / Unidtest IMAT Prep Book
Various Italian publishers
RecommendedMedium
Italian publishers produce IMAT-specific prep books with practice tests. These are the only books that include general knowledge sections matching IMAT's style. Available primarily on Italian Amazon.
Pros
  • Only books with IMAT-specific GK practice
  • Full practice tests included
  • Written specifically for IMAT format
Cons
  • May be partly in Italian
  • Variable quality between publishers
  • Harder to find outside Italy

Books vs. Online Practice: What's More Important?

Based on our survey of high-scoring students, the optimal split is:

  • 40% textbook study — building foundational knowledge
  • 30% past papers — understanding the exam format and timing
  • 20% online practice — targeted question practice with instant feedback
  • 10% general knowledge — reading newspapers, watching documentaries, staying informed

The biggest mistake students make is spending 100% of their time reading books without practicing questions. Books teach you concepts; practice teaches you how to apply them under time pressure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the single best book for IMAT?
There's no single best book since IMAT covers multiple subjects. If you had to pick just one, Campbell Biology is the most impactful because Biology has the highest weightage (18 questions). But you'll need to supplement with chemistry, physics, and logical reasoning resources.
Do I need IMAT-specific books?
Not necessarily. General science textbooks (like Barron's AP series) combined with IMAT past papers work well. The one exception is general knowledge — IMAT-specific books from Italian publishers (EditTest/Unidtest) are the only ones that cover this section properly.
Are free online resources enough?
They can cover 60-70% of preparation. Khan Academy for science, ItaloStudy for practice questions, and YouTube for logical reasoning are excellent free resources. But structured textbooks provide systematic coverage that scattered online resources miss. The ideal is combining 2-3 key books with online practice.
How many months should I study?
4-6 months for a comfortable pace, 2-3 months for an intensive schedule. Students who already took NEET or AP/IB exams need 8-12 weeks since the science syllabus overlaps significantly. Start past papers in the last month regardless of total study time.
