About This Free IMAT Practice Test

This free IMAT practice test is designed to give you a realistic preview of the International Medical Admissions Test. With 15 carefully crafted questions spanning all five IMAT subjects, you can assess your readiness before taking a full-length mock exam.

The test uses the official IMAT scoring formula: +1.5 points for each correct answer, -0.4 points for each wrong answer, and 0 points for questions left blank. This negative marking system means guessing randomly can actually hurt your score — a critical strategic consideration.

How the IMAT Exam Works

The International Medical Admissions Test (IMAT) is the entrance examination for English-taught medical programs at Italian public universities. Here's what you need to know about the format:

  • Total Questions: 60 multiple-choice questions with 5 options each
  • Time Limit: 100 minutes (approximately 1 minute 40 seconds per question)
  • General Knowledge: 12 questions on culture, civics, and current affairs
  • Logical Reasoning: 8 questions testing critical thinking and problem-solving
  • Biology: 18 questions — the largest and most weighted section
  • Chemistry: 12 questions covering general, organic, and inorganic chemistry
  • Physics & Mathematics: 10 questions on mechanics, thermodynamics, and algebra

This practice test maintains the same proportional distribution, scaling down to 15 questions in 25 minutes so you can quickly gauge your strengths and weaknesses.

IMAT Scoring Formula Explained

Your IMAT score is calculated using a formula that rewards knowledge and penalizes random guessing:

Score = (Correct × 1.5) + (Wrong × -0.4) + (Blank × 0)

Maximum possible score: 90 points (60 correct × 1.5). Minimum possible score: -24 points (60 wrong × -0.4). The negative marking means you should leave a question blank if you cannot eliminate at least 2 of the 5 options.

When to Guess vs. Leave Blank

The IMAT's negative marking creates an important strategic decision: when should you guess? Here's the math:

  • Random guess (5 options): Expected value = (1/5 × 1.5) + (4/5 × -0.4) = 0.3 - 0.32 = -0.02 → slightly negative, don't guess randomly
  • Eliminate 1 option (4 remaining): Expected value = (1/4 × 1.5) + (3/4 × -0.4) = 0.375 - 0.3 = +0.075 → marginally worth it
  • Eliminate 2 options (3 remaining): Expected value = (1/3 × 1.5) + (2/3 × -0.4) = 0.5 - 0.267 = +0.233 → definitely guess

The takeaway: if you can confidently eliminate at least 2 wrong answers, take the guess. Otherwise, leave it blank.

How to Prepare for the IMAT

Effective IMAT preparation requires a balanced approach across all five subjects. Here's what top-scoring students recommend:

  1. Start with Biology — it's worth the most points (18 questions × 1.5 = 27 possible points). Focus on cell biology, genetics, anatomy, and evolution.
  2. Master Logical Reasoning — these 8 questions are the most improvable with practice. Learn to identify argument structures, syllogisms, and numerical patterns.
  3. Use Past Papers — work through all available IMAT past papers under timed conditions. The question style and difficulty level are consistent year to year.
  4. Practice with Mock Tests — full-length simulations build exam stamina and time management skills. ItaloStudy offers unlimited mock exams with detailed analytics.
  5. Review Strategically — after each practice session, spend equal time reviewing your wrong answers. Understanding why you made mistakes is more valuable than doing more questions.
