Why Pakistani Students Are Choosing Italy for Medicine

Pakistan is now the second-largest source of IMAT applicants from South Asia, with over 1,800 Pakistani students applying annually. The appeal is clear: Italian public universities offer a WHO-recognized, English-taught MD degree at a fraction of the cost of private medical colleges in Pakistan.

While private MBBS programs in Pakistan can cost PKR 20-50 lakh per year (with total costs reaching PKR 1-2.5 crore over 5 years), an Italian public university charges between PKR 50,000 and PKR 12 lakh per year in tuition — with many students from lower-income families paying virtually nothing through income-based fee waivers.

The Italian MD is a 6-year program taught entirely in English, recognised across the European Union, and listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS). Unlike many countries that Pakistani students consider (China, Russia, Ukraine, Kyrgyzstan), Italy offers a globally portable, EU-standard medical degree from some of the world's oldest and most prestigious universities.

Key Fact for Pakistani Students

You do NOT need MDCAT to study medicine in Italy. The IMAT (International Medical Admissions Test) is completely independent of Pakistani entrance exams. FSc Pre-Medical or A-Level (with Biology, Chemistry, and Physics/Math) qualifies you directly.

Eligibility for Pakistani Students

  • FSc Pre-Medical or A-Levels — with Biology, Chemistry, and Physics or Mathematics. Minimum 60% marks recommended.
  • Valid passport — Must be valid for at least 18 months. Machine-readable passport (MRP) required.
  • Age requirement — At least 17 years old by December 31 of the admission year.
  • No IELTS required — IMAT is in English; passing it proves English competence. A few universities may ask for B2 certificate.
  • Universitaly pre-enrollment — Register on universitaly.it portal before the deadline (typically July).
  • Attestation from IBCC — Inter Board Committee of Chairmen attestation of your FSc result is required for A-Level holders to convert their qualification.

Scholarship Opportunities for Pakistani Students

Several scholarship options are available specifically for Pakistani students or for all international students in Italy:

DSU (Diritto allo Studio Universitario) Scholarship

Available at all Italian public universities for students with ISEE equivalent below €23,000. Covers tuition fee waiver, free accommodation, free canteen meals, and annual book allowance.

€5,000 — €6,000/year (~PKR 15-18 lakh)

Italian Government Scholarships (MAECI)

The Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs offers scholarships to Pakistani nationals for university study. Covers tuition, monthly stipend, and health insurance. Apply through the Italian Embassy in Islamabad.

€900/month stipend (~PKR 2.7 lakh/month)

University Merit Scholarships

Several Italian universities offer merit-based scholarships to top IMAT scorers. Universities like Bologna, Padua, and Milan have specific international student scholarship programs.

€1,000 — €5,000/year tuition reduction
HEC Scholarships: While HEC scholarships for undergraduate study abroad are limited, check the HEC portal regularly for any new programs covering Italy. Some Pakistani students have successfully used partial HEC funding combined with Italian fee waivers.

Step-by-Step Application Process from Pakistan

1

Start Preparation (January - April 2027)

Begin IMAT preparation alongside your FSc exams or after A-Levels. Focus on Biology and Chemistry from your FSc syllabus — the overlap with IMAT is significant. Add Logical Reasoning and General Knowledge practice using ItaloStudy's question bank.

2

Get Documents Attested (April - May 2027)

Get your FSc result attested by IBCC (Inter Board Committee of Chairmen). If you have A-Levels, get the equivalence certificate from IBCC. Also begin the process for your Dichiarazione di Valore (Declaration of Value) through the Italian Embassy in Islamabad.

3

Universitaly Pre-Enrollment (May - July 2027)

Register on universitaly.it, select your preferred universities (up to 3 choices), upload documents including passport, marksheet, and photo. The pre-enrollment deadline is typically in July.

4

IMAT Registration & Exam (July - September 2027)

Register on Cambridge Assessment's portal when registration opens. The exam fee is approximately €110 (~PKR 33,000). Take the exam in September — either online or at designated centres.

5

Results & Enrollment (October 2027)

Results come within 2-3 weeks. Universities publish rankings based on IMAT scores and your preference list. If assigned a seat, confirm enrollment and pay the first tuition instalment.

6

Student Visa Application (October - November 2027)

Apply for a Type D student visa at the Italian Embassy in Islamabad or the Consulate in Karachi. You need your acceptance letter, proof of funds (€6,500+, ~PKR 20 lakh in bank), health insurance, accommodation proof, and valid passport. Processing takes 3-6 weeks.

7

Travel to Italy (November - December 2027)

Book flights from Islamabad, Lahore, or Karachi to your university city. Upon arrival, apply for your Permesso di Soggiorno (residence permit) within 8 days, get your Codice Fiscale (tax code), and set up an Italian bank account.

HEC Equivalence & Recognition

One of the most important considerations for Pakistani students is ensuring their Italian degree is recognized back home:

  • WDOMS Listing: All 14 Italian public universities offering English-taught medicine are listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS). Verify your chosen university before applying.
  • HEC Equivalence: After graduation, you can apply to the Higher Education Commission (HEC) Pakistan for equivalence of your Italian MD degree. The 6-year Italian MD is equivalent to the Pakistani MBBS.
  • PMDC/PMC Licensing: To practice in Pakistan, you'll need to pass the PMC (Pakistan Medical Commission) licensing exam. Italian-trained graduates generally perform well due to the rigorous European training standard.
  • EU Practice: Alternatively, your Italian MD allows you to practice anywhere in the EU without additional licensing exams — many Pakistani graduates choose to build careers in Italy, Germany, UK, or other European countries.

Pakistani Student Community in Italy

Italy has a well-established Pakistani community, particularly in major cities. Pakistani medical students benefit from:

  • Pakistani Student Associations — Active groups in Milan, Rome, Bologna, and Turin that organise study sessions, cultural events, and newcomer orientation.
  • Halal food availability — Halal shops, Pakistani/South Asian grocery stores, and halal restaurants are available in all major Italian cities. Many students cook together to save money.
  • Mosques and prayer facilities — Mosques exist in Milan, Rome, Turin, Bologna, Naples, and most university cities. Jummah prayers are well-attended.
  • WhatsApp support groups — Senior Pakistani students maintain city-specific WhatsApp groups to help newcomers with accommodation, bank setup, and daily life.
  • Cultural celebrations — Eid ul-Fitr, Eid ul-Adha, Pakistan Day, and other occasions are celebrated collectively by the Pakistani community.
  • Money transfer services — Sending money between Pakistan and Italy is easy through services like Western Union, Wise (TransferWise), and bank transfers.

Frequently Asked Questions — Pakistani Students

Can Pakistani students study medicine in Italy?
Yes. Pakistani students can study medicine in Italy through English-taught MD programs at public universities. The entry requirement is passing the IMAT (International Medical Admissions Test). Pakistani FSc Pre-Medical or A-Level qualifications are accepted. No MDCAT score is needed for Italian universities.
What is the cost of studying medicine in Italy for Pakistani students?
Italian public universities charge €150-€4,000 per year in tuition based on family income, which translates to approximately PKR 50,000 to PKR 12 lakh per year. With living expenses of €500-€800/month (PKR 1.5-2.5 lakh/month), the total 6-year cost is approximately PKR 80-120 lakh — far cheaper than private medical colleges in Pakistan.
Is an Italian medical degree recognized by PMDC?
Italian medical degrees from universities listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS) are recognized internationally. To practice in Pakistan, graduates must pass the PMC licensing examination. The 6-year Italian MD is equivalent to the Pakistani MBBS. HEC equivalence can also be obtained.
Do Pakistani students need IELTS for Italy?
No. Since IMAT is conducted entirely in English, most Italian universities do not require a separate English proficiency test like IELTS or TOEFL. Passing IMAT itself serves as proof of English competence. However, a few universities may request a B2 English certificate.
How do I get a student visa for Italy from Pakistan?
After IMAT admission, apply for a Type D student visa at the Italian Embassy in Islamabad or the Consulate in Karachi. Required documents include the university acceptance letter, proof of funds (€6,500+, approximately PKR 20 lakh in bank), health insurance, accommodation proof, and passport. Processing takes 3-6 weeks.
