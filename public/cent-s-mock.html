FAQs

Everything you need to know about the 2026 admissions cycle and ItaloStudy.

What is the CEnT-S Mock Test?

The CEnT-S Mock Test is an official practice simulation designed to replicate the real CEnT-S entrance exam format, timing, and difficulty.

How many sections are in the CEnT-S exam?

The exam consists of multiple sections including Logic, Reading Comprehension, Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Physics.

Are the mock tests timed?

Yes, our mock tests run on a strict timer that perfectly mimics the actual exam conditions.

When will results be published?

Results and detailed analytics are typically published immediately upon completion or at a scheduled time for live cohort mocks.

How does the scoring system work?

Points are awarded for correct answers, zero for unanswered questions, and fractional penalties apply for incorrect answers, matching official rules.

Can I review past papers?

Yes, the historical archive section provides access to past mocks and official papers for review.

Is there a negative marking in CEnT-S?

Yes, standard negative marking is applied to penalize guessing, exactly as in the real exam.

Are these mocks updated for the 2026 syllabus?

Absolutely. All content is strictly aligned with the latest official 2026 CEnT-S ministerial decrees.

Do I need an account to take the mock?

Yes, a free account is required to securely save your answers, track your progress, and compare scores.

Is there a fee for the official CEnT-S mocks?

We offer one free mock test in the Explorer plan. Unlimited mock tests are included in our premium Global plan.
